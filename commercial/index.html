Restaurant and Food Service Water Heaters

Restaurants and food-service businesses have hot water demands that ordinary equipment can't meet. Conroe Water Heater handles food-service hot water, including high-recovery gas tanks, tankless racks, booster heaters for high-temperature dish machines, correct sizing based on peak-hour demand, and scheduled service built around your hours.

Our technicians know how to work in occupied facilities, installing after close when needed, keeping downtime short, and staging temporary hot water where possible.

Safe Installation

Safe Installations, Documented Service

Every commercial job we take on starts with safety. We inspect venting and draft, verify adequate combustion air, check relief valves and their discharge lines, confirm expansion tanks are charged correctly, and adjust mixing valves so water stored at higher temperatures leaves the tap at a safe temperature. Permits and inspections are handled as part of the job. Findings go into a written report with priority levels and specific recommended actions.

Regular professional service protects your people and your property, and following through on our recommendations helps keep small issues from becoming liabilities. It also creates a record. We maintain service logs, temperature records, and a documentation binder for every account, and we increase visit frequency for heavy-use equipment in kitchens, laundries, and central plant rooms. That record shows landlords, insurers, and inspectors that you've done your due diligence.
