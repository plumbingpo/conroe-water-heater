When to Call for Commercial Water Heaters

Most tanks announce trouble weeks or months ahead with rumbling noises, rusty water, or hot showers that keep getting shorter. These are the red flags our technicians see most often before a breakdown:

  • Higher utility bills that point to a heater losing efficiency
  • A commercial heater that has grown noticeably louder
  • Rusty water or damp spots around the equipment
  • Scalding bursts or wild temperature swings at showers or sinks
  • Long waits for hot water at sinks far from the heater
  • Lukewarm water at the exact time you need the most
  • An aging unit you would rather replace on your schedule than in an emergency
  • Trouble reaching sanitation temperatures in the kitchen

Simple Ways to Extend Your Water Heater's Life

A few simple habits, like an annual flush and a quick look at the relief valve, can add years to a tank. Follow these tips from our technicians:

  • Record fixture temperatures regularly, especially before inspections
  • Budget for replacement as equipment nears the end of its expected life
  • Do not store supplies against the equipment or block air openings
  • Set a flushing schedule based on usage; busy tanks collect sediment fast
  • Verify delivery temperatures at sinks and showers a few times a year
  • Listen for a failing recirculation pump and have check valves inspected
  • Check anodes on a schedule matched to your volume and water quality
